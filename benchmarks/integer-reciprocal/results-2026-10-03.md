# Integer reciprocal range proof

The new producer makes `scalar / compact integer` faster while retaining binary64 division and the existing storage decision. In the original 34-case panel, the two int16 reciprocal cases improve **1.59× without missing inputs and 1.41× with sparse missing inputs**. They still cost **1.30× and 1.54× typed-double arithmetic**. The separate long reciprocal screen improves 1.32× and 1.21×, reaching 1.07× and 1.14× typed double. These are gains on the measured cases, not universal parity.

Wall-time measurements agree with the treatment CPU improvements:

| Matrix | Denominator | Missing inputs | Wall before, ms | Wall after, ms | Wall speedup |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Original 34 cases | int16 | none | 0.659 | 0.415 | 1.587× |
| Original 34 cases | int16 | sparse | 0.655 | 0.461 | 1.420× |
| Separate screen | long | none | 0.427 | 0.325 | 1.314× |
| Separate screen | long | sparse | 0.425 | 0.354 | 1.200× |

After the existing storage preflight selects float or double output, every nonzero byte/int16/int32 denominator has magnitude at least one and converts exactly to binary64. A valid scalar within the chosen output range therefore bounds every rounded quotient. The producer checks that bound once, substitutes a safe denominator for missing/zero lanes before division, and writes system missing for those lanes. Its cached missing count is the protected captured input count plus observed zeros. Temporal columns, floating denominators, integer outputs, and unsafe scalars retain the existing producer. Storage preflight and whole-column promotion remain unchanged.

The actual-header diagnostic preserves its failing baseline: 64 cases have no semantic mismatch, but 46 violate the proposed work bound. The candidate passes all 64. On the million-row fractional fixtures, the original storage preflight still visits 16,384 rows; late-fractional fixtures can still require a full preflight. The candidate removes the generic per-output validity/fit reductions from the admitted producer. It still loads and writes every lane. These are structural work counts, not an attribution of CPU time to individual checks.

The final matrix has 1,224 observations: 34 cases, three representations, two clean builds and six balanced rounds. Inputs and operations are the original panel. Each typed result is checked against an independent binary64/Stata normalization and storage-rounding oracle, then every expected missing result is cleared outside the measured interval; the complete cleared values must agree, and `anyNA(cleared_result)` must be false. Both builds must enter the native route. Result allocation and automatic GC are timed; construction, explicit GC, calibration, hashing and mutation qualification are excluded. The CPU intervals actually observed span 94–189 ms. Both runs bind the exact source, installed libraries, R runtime and controllers before and after. Tables use medians of per-call times and ratios of those medians; paired speedups instead take the median of six within-round ratios.

The separate 432-observation screen uses the unchanged unscaled integer denominator grid for int16 and long. It contains four reciprocal treatments and eight unchanged controls, plus typed-double and bare controls. Byte denominators, all 27 missing tags, signed zero, imported physical endpoints, retained chunks, late promotion and reentrant mutation are correctness-test scope rather than additional throughput populations.

Control behavior prevents an unqualified no-regression claim. The initial missing-free float `x * 1.01` control slowdown does not repeat in the full panel: 1.001× direct and 0.998× paired speedup. Its sparse counterpart is slower again: 0.964× direct and 0.974× paired speedup, with five of six paired rounds slower. All 32 unchanged controls span 0.961–1.060× direct and 0.974–1.053× paired speedup. The complete emitted float-scalar writer is identical across the measured binaries after two verified symbol relocations, covering 22,144 instructions. That rules out a changed writer instruction sequence; it does not identify effects from the caller, addresses, allocation history, collection state or run order.

Typed and bare controls have different contracts. Compact int16 output may be rounded once to float; typed-double results stay double. Typed division turns invalid results into system missing; bare R division can retain infinity. Full hashes are compared within each representation against its corresponding oracle, not falsely across these distinct output policies.
