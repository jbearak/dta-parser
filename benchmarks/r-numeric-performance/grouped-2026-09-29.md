# Grouped plain and owned vectors in dibbles, 2026-09-29

> Historical run under the former foreign-write isolation contract. The [explicit-copy contract results](grouped-2026-09-29-explicit-copy.md) supersede the current-performance conclusions below. These original observations are retained.

All 20 measured grouped cases now beat dplyr and take at most 1.05 times data.table's runtime. Each comparison also meets those thresholds at the upper end of its pointwise 95% bootstrap interval. Median speedups against the completed plain-vector branch range from 16.82 to 117.36 times. Native publication counters confirm that all 20 cases use the intended paths.

Reassessing grouping after the [plain-vector changes](plain-2026-09-29.md) found a separate admission bottleneck: the grouped paths accepted compact integer keys but rejected ordinary and owned double keys. [Source research](../../docs/research/grouped-double-key-admission-2026-09-29.md) compared the existing capture phases with dplyr, data.table and vctrs. The change adds direct snapshots of canonical plain or owned double keys, preserving each caller's capture and final validation order. Public dependency guards, metadata checks, frozen outputs and ordinary continuation remain in place.

These results cover source-plus-constant creation, with one or five outputs. They do not establish the same performance for grouped replacement, arbitrary expressions, missing or fractional group keys, custom metadata, or other runtime builds. The bracket and mutate paths admit `source + 1` with positive integral keys up to INT_MAX; gen retains literal offsets and its existing signed integral key domain. Unsupported cases continue through ordinary execution. All inputs in this benchmark use plain or owned Stata double vectors, including every sibling and key; compact vectors are excluded.

| Inputs | Cases | Beats dplyr | At most 5% slower than data.table | Literal ±5% | Faster than plain-vector branch |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Plain | 10 | 10 | 10 | 1 | 10 |

Literal ±5% means 0.95–1.05 times the comparator's runtime. Nineteen cases are more than 5% faster than data.table. Wide plain `mutate()` still copies ordinary buffers to isolate later foreign writes; at 10,000 rows its cost fits within the measured target. The 100,000-row wide ungrouped `mutate()` limitation in the plain report remains.

## Validation and measurement

The full installed suite passed 26,874 assertions, with zero failures, errors or skips and seven pre-existing warnings. After tightening the rejection order for unfamiliar ALTREP inputs and adding the range regression, the final affected-file run passed 16 assertions with no failures, errors, skips or warnings. The report distinguishes these runs: [full observations](grouped-2026-09-29/full-test-results.csv), [final targeted observations](grouped-2026-09-29/final-targeted-test-results.csv).

The new child-process regressions exercise both representations, narrow and wide mutate inputs, positive native publication, complete values and attributes, missing and fractional key fallback, first-appearance groups, key writes during snapshot and validation, public equality/arithmetic/cast traces, foreign writes between assignments, later input/output alias isolation and Stata range normalization. Existing compact-key regressions remain required. Native CI's optional-dependency and child-process policies are updated.

Two fresh R processes ran candidate then the plain-vector baseline, with seeds 290929 and 290930. There are 20 observations for each of 36 engine/condition combinations, 720 calls per build and 1,440 total. Engines are randomized within each condition. Every call passed complete value, column order, target and untouched attribute checks outside timing. Full public calls are timed; fixtures, explicit GC, admission counters and verification are outside the timer. Candidate had zero timed collections; baseline had 118, included in its timings. data.table uses one thread. No local build or test ran concurrently with timing.

The unchanged analyzer uses fixed seed 20260929 and 10,000 bootstrap resamples. Candidate/comparator observations are paired within each sample; builds are resampled independently. The legacy `main_*` CSV fields mean the plain-vector baseline in this report. Intervals are pointwise percentile intervals, not simultaneous guarantees or a formal equivalence test. One machine, one process per build and candidate-first build order limit generalization. [Complete comparisons](grouped-2026-09-29/comparisons.csv), [saved indices](grouped-2026-09-29/bootstrap-indices.npz), [summary](grouped-2026-09-29/summary.json).

Measured on macOS ARM64, R 4.6.1 revision 90187, dplyr 1.2.1, data.table 1.18.6.1, vctrs 0.7.3, rlang 1.3.0 and tibble 3.3.1. The guarded paths require the audited dependency artifacts; other builds retain ordinary fallback. The source and DLL hashes pin both measured builds. Later rebases change only CI and test ancestry. [Source and DLL hashes](grouped-2026-09-29/provenance.json), [candidate session](grouped-2026-09-29/experiments-session.txt), [baseline session](grouped-2026-09-29/merged-main-session.txt), [candidate observations](grouped-2026-09-29/experiments-raw.csv), [baseline observations](grouped-2026-09-29/merged-main-raw.csv).
