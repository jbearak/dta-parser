# Plain vectors in dibbles, 2026-09-29

All 56 measured ungrouped plain-vector cases improved against #266, by 1.74–18.92 times at the median. In 51 cases, dtatools beat dplyr and took no more than 1.05 times data.table's runtime. Both thresholds also hold at the upper end of each case's pointwise 95% bootstrap interval. The remaining five cases are copy-returning `mutate()` on 100,000-row, 100-column inputs. This is evidence for the measured cases on the audited runtime, not a guarantee for all operations or builds.

The change lets canonical ordinary double buffers enter the existing guarded native operations. Fresh outputs are owned. Public casts, arithmetic, math and helper bindings are qualified before native publication; callbacks or unsupported inputs retain ordinary execution. The [upstream research](../../docs/research/plain-and-grouped-upstream-implementations-2026-09-29.md) explains the comparison with dplyr's result construction and data.table's assignment implementation.

Every dtatools operation below runs on a dibble. Every input column, including untouched columns and grouping keys, uses the stated plain or owned double representation. Compact vectors are excluded. Comparators use plain doubles in ordinary tibbles and data.tables. Literal ±5% excludes cases more than 5% faster than data.table.

The five wide plain `mutate()` cases take 3.08–4.55 times dplyr and 6.60–11.37 times data.table. Preserving isolation from future foreign by-reference writes requires copying the 100 ordinary buffers, about 80 MB here. Owned buffers can be forked through copy-on-write. Simply sharing the ordinary buffers would violate the package's alias contract. The reference operations avoid that whole-table copy and meet the comparator thresholds throughout this matrix.

Owned performance remains close to the #266 baseline, with median ratios spanning a 3.44% slowdown to an 11.51% improvement. Its four `replace_values(x = abs(-3))` cases still miss the data.table target. All 20 grouped cases still miss both targets; grouped changes belong to the next PR.

## Validation and measurement

The final executable build passed the complete installed suite: 26,869 assertions, no failures, errors or skips, and seven pre-existing warnings. Regressions cover positive admission, values and full attribute order, tagged missing values, custom metadata, public tracing, pending RHS continuation, real finalizer writes before and after capture, and later foreign writes in both directions. [Test observations](plain-2026-09-29/test-results.csv).

`validate-plain-grouped.R` strengthens the previous protocol by checking the complete target attribute list as well as untouched columns and values. The matrix contains four ungrouped shapes, 100 or 100,000 rows at 2 or 100 columns, and two grouped shapes, 10,000 rows and 100 groups at 2 or 100 columns. Grouped scenarios cover one and five creations, not grouped replacement. Five-output calls repeat `x + 1`.

Two fresh processes ran candidate then baseline, with 20 randomized within-condition observations per engine, 4,560 calls per build and 9,120 total. All calls passed verification outside the timer. Full public calls and any timed GC are included; candidate had 118 timed collections, baseline 115. Fixtures, explicit GC and verification are outside timing. data.table uses one thread. There was no concurrent local build or test workload during timing.

The unchanged analyzer uses 10,000 resamples and fixed seed 20260929. Candidate/comparator observations are paired within samples; builds are resampled independently. Intervals are pointwise percentile intervals, not simultaneous guarantees or a formal equivalence test. Build order and one machine remain limitations. [Complete comparisons](plain-2026-09-29/comparisons.csv), [saved bootstrap indices](plain-2026-09-29/bootstrap-indices.npz), [aggregate counts](plain-2026-09-29/summary.json).

Measured on macOS ARM64, R 4.6.1 revision 90187, dplyr 1.2.1, data.table 1.18.6.1, vctrs 0.7.3, rlang 1.3.0 and tibble 3.3.1. Admission remains restricted to the audited dependency artifacts; other builds execute ordinary fallback. Baseline executable source is #266 commit `19384fe0`; later #266 CI and test changes do not alter executable behavior. The source and DLL hashes pin the measured candidate; later rebases change only CI and test ancestry. [Source and DLL hashes](plain-2026-09-29/provenance.json), [candidate session](plain-2026-09-29/experiments-session.txt), [baseline session](plain-2026-09-29/merged-main-session.txt), [candidate raw observations](plain-2026-09-29/experiments-raw.csv), [baseline raw observations](plain-2026-09-29/merged-main-raw.csv).
