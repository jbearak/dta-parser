# DTA writer qualification and corpus benchmark

Run date: 2026-08-27.

Environment: Apple M4 Max, 128 GB RAM, macOS 26.5.2, R 4.6.1,
dtatools 0.5.0 built with Rust 1.98.0, and Stata/MP 18. This report is
aggregate-only: it publishes no private paths, labels, or values from
`/opt/aww_cache`.

## Qualification

The hash-bound inventory contained 1,823 DTA files. All 1,821 files readable
by the existing parser passed semantic read-write-read comparison, Haven
dimension comparison, and a Stata 18 open-and-dimension check. The two
previously recorded, SHA-256-bound unreadable MICS inputs remained the only
exclusions. A generated 32,768-variable file also passed and exercised release
119.

| Corpus | Passed | Expected exclusions |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| DHS | 643 | 0 |
| MICS | 949 | 2 |
| NSFG | 229 | 0 |
| Total | 1,821 | 2 |

The inventory SHA-256 is
`077b9c7474a3af2ce5f7b488a81a49952e022af032315080f27e50cad9dc6c49`.
The qualified installed-package SHA-256 is
`6b5f7c4b910ba6dc6bb1e2d5dd377358dc13a6d4e3c7e27109202aec818d5549`.

## Corpus benchmark

After qualification, the same package build and inventory completed 1,822
dtatools writes and 1,822 Stata writes: the 1,821 qualified inputs plus the
wide release-119 case. The table below covers the real corpus inputs, totaling
56.430 GB. Each input was measured once in a fresh process, with writer order
alternating across inputs.

Elapsed time covers only `save_dta()` or Stata's `save`, after the input was
loaded. Peak RSS covers the complete process, including its in-memory input.
Times are sums; peak RSS is the largest fresh-process value in the group.

| Corpus | Writer | Files | Total write time | Peak RSS | Output |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| DHS | dtatools | 643 | 1,223.129 s | 5.570 GB | 47.740 GB |
| DHS | Stata | 643 | 9.174 s | 5.268 GB | 47.827 GB |
| MICS | dtatools | 949 | 101.304 s | 0.317 GB | 3.764 GB |
| MICS | Stata | 949 | 0.875 s | 0.112 GB | 3.731 GB |
| NSFG | dtatools | 229 | 122.871 s | 0.703 GB | 5.929 GB |
| NSFG | Stata | 229 | 1.500 s | 0.452 GB | 5.912 GB |
| Total | dtatools | 1,821 | 1,447.304 s | 5.570 GB | 57.433 GB |
| Total | Stata | 1,821 | 11.549 s | 5.268 GB | 57.470 GB |

These are warm-cache, machine- and corpus-specific measurements, not
performance guarantees. The content-bound private bundle is retained at
`target/r-corpus-roundtrip/20260827T132220Z`; its raw table disaggregates the
results by source release. Haven was deliberately omitted from the corpus
writes because that would add a costly third pass without strengthening the
qualification gate. It is included in the repeated synthetic comparison in
[`../large-scale/results-2026-08-27.md`](../large-scale/results-2026-08-27.md).
