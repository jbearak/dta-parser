# DTA writer qualification and corpus benchmark

Run date: 2026-08-28.

Environment: Apple M4 Max (Mac16,9), 128 GB RAM, macOS 26.5.2, R 4.6.1,
dtaparser 0.6.0 built with Rust 1.98.0, and Stata/MP 18. This report is
aggregate-only: it publishes no private corpus paths, filenames, stable
per-file identifiers, labels, values, or raw rows.

## Qualification

The hash-bound inventory contained 1,823 DTA files. All 1,821 files readable
by the existing parser passed semantic read-write-read comparison, Haven
dimension comparison, and a Stata/MP 18 open-and-dimension check. The two
previously recorded, SHA-256-bound unreadable MICS inputs remained the only
exclusions. A generated 32,768-variable file also passed and exercised release
119.

| Corpus | Passed | Expected exclusions |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| DHS | 643 | 0 |
| MICS | 949 | 2 |
| NSFG | 229 | 0 |
| Total | 1,821 | 2 |

The complete gate therefore finished with 1,821 passes, two expected
exclusions, zero failures, and `synthetic_wide = pass`.

## Executable interoperability scope

Stata/MP 18 performed every executable open-and-dimension check and the
release-119 wide-file check. A separate Stata 19 executable run was
intentionally descoped: this host has Stata/MP 18, while the writer's Stata 18
and Stata 19 targets use the shared release-118 and release-119 file formats.

## Corpus benchmark

After qualification, the same installed package and inventory completed 1,822
dtaparser writes and 1,822 Stata writes: the 1,821 qualified inputs plus the
wide release-119 case. The table below covers the real corpus inputs, totaling
56.430 GB. Each input was measured once in a fresh process, with writer order
alternating across inputs.

Elapsed time covers only `write_dta()` or Stata's `save`, after the input was
loaded. Peak RSS covers the complete process, including its in-memory input.
Times are sums; peak RSS is the largest fresh-process value in the group.

| Corpus | Writer | Files | Total write time | Peak RSS | Output |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| DHS | dtaparser | 643 | 695.686 s | 5.240 GB | 47.740 GB |
| DHS | Stata | 643 | 10.170 s | 5.268 GB | 47.827 GB |
| MICS | dtaparser | 949 | 45.756 s | 0.252 GB | 3.764 GB |
| MICS | Stata | 949 | 0.955 s | 0.112 GB | 3.731 GB |
| NSFG | dtaparser | 229 | 75.086 s | 0.703 GB | 5.929 GB |
| NSFG | Stata | 229 | 1.604 s | 0.452 GB | 5.912 GB |
| Total | dtaparser | 1,821 | 816.528 s | 5.240 GB | 57.433 GB |
| Total | Stata | 1,821 | 12.729 s | 5.268 GB | 57.470 GB |

The generated wide case produced a 21.169 MB release-119 file with both
writers. Its measured write time and peak process RSS were 1.703 seconds and
0.195 GB for dtaparser, and 0.002 seconds and 0.322 GB for Stata.

These are warm-cache, machine- and corpus-specific measurements, not
performance guarantees. The content-bound private bundle is retained at
`target/r-corpus-roundtrip/20260828T114127Z-94234.jkKXGf`; its raw tables and
worker outputs remain ignored and unpublished. Haven was deliberately omitted
from the corpus writes because a third complete pass would not strengthen the
qualification gate. It remains part of the repeated synthetic comparison in
[`../large-scale/results-2026-08-28.md`](../large-scale/results-2026-08-28.md).
