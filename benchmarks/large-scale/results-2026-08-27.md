# Synthetic DTA writer results

Run date: 2026-08-27.

Environment: Apple M4 Max, 128 GB RAM, macOS 26.5.2, R 4.6.1,
dtaparser 0.5.0, Haven 2.5.5, and Stata/MP 18. The current package source was
built and installed into a private benchmark library before the run.

The suite measured dtaparser, Stata, and Haven writes seven times each on
deterministic approximately 100 MB and 1 GB inputs. Every writer ran in a fresh
process, and writer order rotated. The operation timer began after that
writer's own reader loaded the input. Peak RSS covers the whole process,
including the in-memory input; it is therefore a workflow memory measurement,
not an isolated writer-allocation measurement.

| Input | Writer | Median time | 5th--95th percentile | Median peak RSS | Median output |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 100 MB | dtaparser | 2.825 s | 2.814--2.852 s | 0.386 GB | 100.000 MB |
| 100 MB | Stata | 0.012 s | 0.012--0.013 s | 0.155 GB | 100.000 MB |
| 100 MB | Haven | 0.474 s | 0.470--0.491 s | 0.255 GB | 113.359 MB |
| 1 GB | dtaparser | 27.034 s | 26.920--27.081 s | 1.704 GB | 1.000 GB |
| 1 GB | Stata | 0.116 s | 0.115--0.121 s | 1.145 GB | 1.000 GB |
| 1 GB | Haven | 4.310 s | 4.305--4.341 s | 0.909 GB | 1.134 GB |

On these inputs, dtaparser's median write time was 5.96 times Haven's at
100 MB and 6.27 times Haven's at 1 GB. Stata was much faster than both R
workflows. The measurement says where optimization work remains; it is not a
correctness gate or a cross-machine performance guarantee.
