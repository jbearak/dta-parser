All compact byte targets remained unmaterialized. Keeping an unselected
missing value at the end of the target had no material timing penalty, which
guards the missing-cache path against a hidden full-column scan. The
clear-and-restore timing also stayed flat while the exact missing count moved
between zero and one. The large sparse patch matched the 50,000-row patch, and
the direct target made no R allocation above the profiler's 1,000-byte
threshold. Metadata-proxy isolation copied exactly one compact byte payload,
12.5% of a full double column. The second proxy patch made no R
allocation above the profiler's 1,000-byte threshold. All-row scalar
replacement covered five million rows in 0.040 seconds, close to compact
scalar generation's 0.038 seconds; scalar validation happened once rather than
once per selected row. Generation's largest allocation was one byte-width
payload, also 12.5% of a full double column. It kept both existing column
objects at their original `tracemem()` addresses; only the new column and
shallow dataset state were added.
