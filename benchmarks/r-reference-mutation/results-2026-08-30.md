All compact byte targets remained unmaterialized. Keeping an unselected
missing value at the end of the target had no material timing penalty, which
guards the missing-cache path against a hidden full-column scan. The
clear-and-restore timing also stayed flat while the exact missing count moved
between zero and one. The large sparse patch matched the 50,000-row patch, and
the direct target made no R allocation above the profiler's 1,000-byte
threshold. Metadata-proxy isolation copied exactly one compact byte payload,
12.5% of a full double column. The second proxy patch made no R
allocation above the profiler's 1,000-byte threshold. Pre-encoding let an
all-row scalar replacement cover five million rows in 0.0002 seconds, below
the independent raw-vector fill timing and without repeated decoding or range
checks. A sparse full-length logical selector took 0.0138 seconds, while a
genuine compact `dta_long` position sequence took 0.1313 seconds; neither
planner made an R allocation above the profiler threshold. Full integer and
compact replacement vectors took 0.050 and 0.077 seconds without a full-vector
R allocation. Scalar and compact-vector generation allocated one byte-width
payload. Integer-vector generation allocated its required four-byte float
payload, not an eight-byte double temporary. Existing columns stayed at their
original `tracemem()` addresses; generation added only the new column and
shallow dataset state.
