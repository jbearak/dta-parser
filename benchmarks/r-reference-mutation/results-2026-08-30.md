All compact byte targets remained unmaterialized. Keeping an unselected
missing value at the end of the target had no material timing penalty, which
guards the missing-cache path against a hidden full-column scan. The
clear-and-restore timing also stayed flat while the exact missing count moved
between zero and one. The largest direct-target allocation was 0.13% of one
compact byte payload and 0.017% of a full double column. Metadata-proxy
isolation copied exactly one compact byte
payload, 12.5% of a full double column. The second proxy patch made no R
allocation above the profiler's 1,000-byte threshold. Compact scalar
generation's largest allocation was one byte-width payload, also 12.5% of a
full double column. It kept both existing column objects at their original
`tracemem()` addresses; only the new column and shallow dataset state were
added.
