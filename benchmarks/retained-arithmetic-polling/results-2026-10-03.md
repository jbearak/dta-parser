# Poll retained arithmetic by completed rows

Long/float addition checked for R interrupts once per retained span. With synthetic 7/11-row chunk geometry, one million rows caused 220,780 checks. The accepted candidate keeps the original complete span requests and checks before processing a span that would exceed 16,384 completed rows since the preceding check. The first check remains before the first lookup. Captured read claims keep acquired pointers stable across later checks. This is a completed-row bound, not a wall-clock cancellation guarantee.

The structural probe verifies 62 checks for the measured plain, 8191/16385 and 7/11 geometries, including the final unpolled tail. It independently derives polling positions from complete-span endpoints and verifies the original requested/returned span trace. The two-sided span calls remain 124, 368 and 441,560 respectively. The candidate has zero semantic or structural failures in 18 cases; the old source has zero semantic failures and 14 structural failures. Other geometries may poll earlier and need more than 62 checks.

This is a narrow improvement. The synthetic short-chunk cases improve 1.056–1.139× by the ratio of median CPU times and 1.046–1.119× by paired-round speedups. Wall time improves 1.050–1.137× (paired 1.043–1.115×), reaching 11.07–11.20 ms. CPU results still take 11.05–11.24 ms per million rows, or 33.46–34.70× the plain typed-double control. Ordinary and realistic retained controls do not show a general gain. Plain sparse long+float costs about 5.2% more by median CPU and 4.6% more by paired CPU; its paired typed-normalized cost rises 2.6%. Medium reverse-prefix addition costs about 5.0%/4.5% more, while its normalized cost rises 0.5%. The largest normalized outlier is plain prefix long+float, +4.7% in the median and slower in five of six normalized rounds. Causes of these control differences are not established.

The initial split-budget candidate d02deee2 is retained as an intermediate experiment, not the accepted implementation. It reduced checks but increased medium-geometry span calls from 368 to 490 by splitting spans at check boundaries. The final candidate e1b0278b preserves baseline span geometry and is compared directly against 3de25ecca; its gains are not multiplied by the first candidate's gains. Both builds contain the same all-missing fill, so that shortcut receives no polling credit.

Measured sources are baseline 3de25ecca674a3ffed3618223a011f499383bd57 and candidate e1b0278b1403b4d8fc2d9a2dfc0c4ddaa7cde3a2. The unchanged 116-case no-clock qualification and 696-observation screen use one million rows, both operand orders, ordinary/sparse/dense/prefix/suffix/all-tag patterns where declared, compact/typed-double controls, and bare controls only for missing-free cases. Six fresh-process rounds balance build and representation order. Typed and bare controls use ordinary unchunked storage even in retained panels. Times include public result allocation and automatic GC; fixture construction, calibration, hashing, explicit GC and cache-mutation qualification are outside recorded intervals. Recorded CPU and wall intervals range from 210 to 360 ms; published per-call times divide each actual interval by its recorded repetitions. Every measured call has the expected native-entry count. Full output/storage/missing/source checks and clearing all missing outputs validate cached counts outside timing.

The source-bound focused suite passed 33,726 assertions in 36 blocks, with no failures, errors, skips or warnings. New tests cover 16,383/16,384/16,385 rows, unequal chunk geometry, output/cache/source preservation and reentrant captured-source changes. The allocation checkpoint validates ownership, while the C structural counter validates interrupt cadence. The independent audit replayed all 696 observations, 116 qualifications and 26 summaries and their bindings. Publication integration 58630e99 is based on fixed PR301 head 213ceeee and retains the measured writer byte-for-byte. Its clean installed focused suite passed 34,804 assertions in 39 blocks; its maintained actual-header cadence guard passed 18 cases and is wired into CI. Preserved source-archive conformance passed 106,933 assertions with zero failures/skips and seven existing test warnings. The exact 353 packaged source files match the frozen commit, with 20 explicit build exclusions. R CMD check reports the existing three warnings (macOS linker target, GNU Make usage and compiled non-API references) and two notes. No new performance measurement is attributed to the integration.

The short-chunk layout intentionally stresses native span/lookup overhead. It does not model Arrow reader throughput or prove a fundamental R limit. Typical retained chunks are much closer to ordinary doubles; the residual synthetic gap remains. No Rust lookup, storage policy, arithmetic, missing classification or output rounding changes are included here. Other arithmetic writers still need their own qualification before adopting a shared polling schedule.
